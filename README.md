# WinSwitch

**Your Windows PC, switched from anywhere in the house.** Power profiles, screen, sleep, volume and power off from the tray, and soon from your iPhone or Home Assistant. And it switches itself when you dock, unplug or go to bed.

- **Profiles** with plugged-in and on-battery settings, each made of five plain dials (Screen, Sleep and lid, Battery or speed, Wireless and devices, Sound), with every Windows setting one click underneath
- **Switches by itself** when you plug into a dock, a monitor or a network, or the battery runs low; learn a dock by plugging it in
- **Tweaks** such as Night (screen off sooner, volume down) on a schedule or from the tray
- **Leaves no trace**: everything is saved before the first change; restore any setting or all of them, and uninstalling puts the PC back as it was
- **Plays nicely with Windows**: works through Windows' own power plan, and asks before keeping a change you made in Windows Settings
- Tray-only, starts with Windows, updates itself in the background

Coming next: remote control over MQTT (with Home Assistant discovery) and a phone page for iPhone Shortcuts, a helper for device power settings, and Apps to stop per profile. See [SPEC.md](SPEC.md).

Same family as [WinSqueeze](https://github.com/hazymat/winsqueeze), [WinGCal](https://github.com/hazymat/wingcal) and [WinTapeRecorder](https://github.com/hazymat/wintaperecorder). Free software under the GNU GPL v3.
